FitFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026.10.03  |  Last updated: 2026.10.03

FitFlow is a fitness tracking app with AI-assisted workout plans, community challenges and nutrition logging. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

FitFlow is operated by Dulyana Ranathunga / WE_106, Dambulla, Sri Lanka. For privacy questions, contact us at dewwandiranathunga@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address and sign-in details.
  • Health and fitness data: fitness score, heart rate, sleep, recovery, workout history and goals.
  • Nutrition data: meal entries, calories, macros and photos of meals you choose to scan.
  • Community data: challenges you join, posts, reactions and leaderboard activity.
  • Device and usage data: device model, operating system, app interactions and crash reports.

3. How we use your information

We use your data to provide and personalise workouts, track your progress, enable nutrition logging, run community features, improve performance, fix crashes and keep the service secure. We do not use your health data for advertising.

4. AI processing

FitFlow's AI features analyse your workout history, goals and recent activity to recommend workouts. You stay in control: each recommendation can be accepted, adjusted or skipped. Your data is not used to train AI models. AI recommendations are general fitness guidance and are not medical advice.

5. Community features

Content you share in the community is visible according to your privacy settings. You can choose who sees your activity, report content, and block other users. We may remove content that breaks our community rules.

6. Storage, security and sharing

Data is encrypted in transit, and sensitive health data is encrypted at rest. We share data only with service providers needed to run FitFlow, such as authentication, cloud hosting and crash reporting providers, and only for those purposes. We do not sell your personal data.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app. Associated personal data is then deleted or anonymised within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete and export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. We process health data only with your explicit consent. Under the CCPA you may also request to know what we collect, request deletion, and opt out of any sale or sharing of your information. To use these rights, email dewwandiranathunga@gmail.com.

9. Health regulations

FitFlow is a consumer fitness app and is not a healthcare provider, so HIPAA does not generally apply to it. We still apply strong safeguards to health data, and would put the required agreements in place if FitFlow were ever integrated with healthcare providers.

10. Children

FitFlow is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app and update the date above.
